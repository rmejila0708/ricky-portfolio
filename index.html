How I work

A real content pipeline, end to end

The automated workflow behind the RevUp Now AI blog: one topic goes in, a reviewed, published, socially repurposed post comes out. A human stays in the loop at review.

01

Trigger & topic select

A weekly scheduled job wakes the pipeline headlessly and picks the next unused topic from a maintained content queue.

Windows Task SchedulerPowerShell
02

Write & illustrate

Claude Code writes the full article in the brand's voice and template, then generates a custom hero image to match.

Claude CodeHiggsfield (nano_banana_pro)
03

Publish draft

The finished post and image are pushed to WordPress as a draft through a purpose-built MCP server. No manual copy-paste.

WordPressRevi MCP
04

Quality gate

A deterministic, zero-token validator checks word count, structure, and banned phrases before any human sees the draft.

PowerShell validator
05

Create review task

A ClickUp task is created with the full article as Markdown plus a downloadable, pixel-accurate HTML preview attached.

ClickUp API
06

Notify reviewer

The reviewer gets pinged directly in Microsoft Teams with a one-click link to the review task.

Microsoft TeamsGraph API
07

Approve & repurpose

Once approved, the post goes live and is automatically repurposed into social posts across four platforms.

GoHighLevel automation

Sales and lead workflows I build in GoHighLevel

Leads, calls, bookings, and follow-up run as connected workflows instead of manual reminders. Each lead gets the right next step based on what happened, and nobody has to remember to send it.

Lead intake

Instant reply, then follow-up

A form submission gets an immediate reply. If the lead doesn't respond within 24 hours, a follow-up goes out automatically.

Lead source

Know where every lead came from

Facebook ad links tag each contact with its source, so reporting and follow-up can tell paid leads from organic ones.

Call outcomes

The result of a call sets the next step

After a sales call, the outcome routes the lead to follow-up, nurture, or close. The workflow below is the live version.

Bookings

Reminders and no-show rescue

Booked calls get reminders. If someone misses the call, a rescue sequence follows up to rebook.

Re-engagement

Win-back email sequences

Timed email sequences bring back past leads and customers who went quiet.

Outbound

Multi-step outbound campaigns

Prospect outreach runs as a multi-step sequence inside GoHighLevel, with each step triggered by the one before it.
